Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans any time?

Yes — upgrade instantly, downgrade at the end of your billing period. We'll prorate the difference.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, and ACH bank transfer for annual plans.

Is there a free trial of Pro?

Every restaurant gets 14 days of Pro free when they sign up. No credit card required upfront.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You keep Pro features until the end of your current billing period, then drop to the free Starter plan automatically.
