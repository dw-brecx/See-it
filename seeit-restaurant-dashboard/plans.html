Frequently asked questions

How does per-location billing work?

You pay for each active location. Add a location → we start billing for it. Remove or pause a location → we stop. We prorate automatically.

Can different locations be on different plans?

Yes. Each location's plan is independent. You might have 2 locations on Pro and 1 on Premium — whatever fits.

Is there a free trial?

Every restaurant gets 14 days of Pro free when they sign up. No credit card required upfront. New locations added later get 14 days free too.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel one location or all of them. You keep Pro features until the end of the current billing period, then drop to free Starter automatically.
